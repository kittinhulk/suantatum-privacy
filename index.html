Privacy Policy

Suantatum Uploader · Last updated 18 November 2026

Suantatum Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the plant shop "สวนตาตุ้มไม้ด่าง" (Suantatum Variegated Plants) to publish the shop's own short videos to the shop's own Facebook Page, Instagram account and YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube: the shop's channel name and ID (to confirm the right channel is selected) and permission to upload videos to that channel. Scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly.
  • Facebook / Instagram: the shop's Page ID, Page name and permission to publish posts and reels to that Page and its linked Instagram account.

The tool does not read, collect or store data about viewers, commenters, customers or any other people.

How the data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer, in a private folder that is not shared or uploaded anywhere.
  • Data is used only to upload the shop's own videos and captions. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Suantatum Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Suantatum Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Facebook access can be removed in the Page's Business Integrations settings.
  • To delete stored tokens, the owner deletes the token files on their computer. No copies exist elsewhere.

Contact

Questions: message the Facebook Page สวนตาตุ้มไม้ด่าง.
